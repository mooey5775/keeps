Source: https://www.keepsgame.com/puzzles (fetched 2026-10-20). Each puzzle starts on your turn with two actions, and its solution wins the round. The board positions are only shown as card images on each puzzle page; the numbers in each entry are the pile card counts shown on the page.

## Last Laugh 1
EASY
Yeah, the solution probably involves Last Laugh.
Take a look at your keep.
0
3
2
5
You have enough coinage in your hand (4) to beat the next best of 3. But you need two actions to play them, which would then pass your turn with no guaranteed win. Fortunately, you have Last Laugh in your keep. Here is the solution:
1. Play either one of your cards into your keep.
2. Draw from your (empty) pile, ending the round.
3. With the final action from Last Laugh, play your other card into your keep.
This way you have your 4 coins, and the round is over!

HARD

Maybe you can use that somehow...

3

Here is the only guaranteed win:
1. Use Sanction to draw Last Laugh.
2. Play Last Laugh into your keep. Your turn is over and the left player is forced to end the round, triggering Last Laugh.

## Royal Favor 3
HARD
Notice your keep: You are Shackled. This means you only get ONE action this turn...
Remember that ties go to the tied player most directly left of whoever draws to end the round.
4
3
4
1
For your one and only action: play Resurrection, adding Royal Favor to your hand. Because it is HOT!, it is played immediately. Put it into the keep of the right player. Because they have Interest, they must draw Blunder, a HOT! card which must be played to one's own keep. Then, because Blunder is a coin card, Interest makes them draw again, ending the game.
You and the left player are then tied at 2 coins, but you win the tiebreak as you are closest left of the right player, who drew to end it.
